Getting the app

Not on the App Store yet. Here is where it actually is.

Short version: the Mac side is finished and deployed. The iPhone app is finished too, but shipping it to strangers needs an Apple Developer Program membership that has not been bought yet. Until then it is a build-it-yourself app.

Building it yourself

You need a Mac with Xcode and an Apple ID. A paid account is not required.

  1. Get the source

    git clone https://github.com/0x5446/reins.git
cd reins

  2. Generate the Xcode project

    Your Team ID comes from developer.apple.com/account — a free Apple ID has one. 

    REINS_TEAM_ID=<your team id> xcodegen generate --spec ios/project.yml

  3. Build to your phone

    Open ios/Reins.xcodeproj, plug the phone in, pick it as the destination, and run. The first launch needs Settings → General → VPN & Device Management on the phone to trust your developer certificate.

The seven-day catch

An app signed with a free Apple ID stops launching after seven days. Rebuilding resets the clock and keeps your data — it is an annoyance, not a loss. A paid membership makes it a year.

What the App Store needs

Everything elseDone. Permission strings, encryption export declaration, and the privacy policy are in place.

One purchase unblocks all three. Naming that plainly seemed better than a roadmap that implies work is in progress when what is missing is a decision.
